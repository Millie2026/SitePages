Fact find / investigation process

Key principle: The purpose of a fact find / investigation is to discover all relevant facts in a fair, reasonable and objective manner. Minor issues do not always require a full investigation — but all fact finds must be documented in writing with expectations set going forward.

Step 2
Conduct fact find meeting
  • Contact the individual informally
  • Advise of right to a companion
  • Note any reasonable adjustments needed
  • Prepare open questions in advance
  • Take detailed notes throughout
  • Interview any witnesses separately

⚖️

Consistent process. Not identical outcomes.

Two people can go through exactly the same process and reach different outcomes. People are different, circumstances are different. What must be consistent is how you conduct the fact find and how you record your decision. The outcome is not black and white.

Factors that influence the outcome
📅

Length of service & history

A first concern in four years is very different to a repeated pattern of the same behaviour.

🤝

Acceptance of responsibility

Whether the individual acknowledges what happened and shows genuine insight matters when deciding next steps.

💛

Mitigating circumstances

Personal pressures, health, or staffing levels outside the individual's control may reduce the severity of the response.

🔁

Pattern or isolated incident

A single lapse in judgement is different to a pattern. Has a similar concern been raised before, even informally?

👥

Impact on others

The effect on service users, colleagues, or the organisation is a key factor in deciding whether informal resolution is appropriate.

📚

Training & awareness

Was the individual trained and aware of the relevant policy? Lack of awareness points toward support rather than sanction.

Appropriate where the concern is minor, a first occurrence, or where support and guidance is sufficient to address the issue without formal action.

  • Letter of concern or documented discussion
  • Coaching, mentoring or additional support
  • Further training or shadowing
  • Performance support plan with a clear review date
  • No further action where allegations are unfounded
  • Always confirm the outcome in writing

Even informal outcomes must be documented in writing and a copy given to the individual.

Required where the concern is serious, repeated, or where informal resolution has already been attempted without improvement.

  • Complete the investigation report in full before proceeding to a hearing
  • Type up, sign and share all meeting notes with the individual
  • Issue a formal hearing invitation in writing stating the allegations clearly
  • Confirm the right to be accompanied in writing
  • Follow the disciplinary process guide

Do not proceed to a disciplinary hearing without a completed investigation report.
