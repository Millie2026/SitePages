Fact find meeting invite
You should always inform the colleague before the meeting. Unlike formal hearings, there is no set template for this. The below is a suggested format you can use or adapt.
Hello [Name], I hope you are well. I would like to invite you to attend a meeting scheduled for [DATE] at [TIME]. The meeting will take place at [LOCATION]. I would like to meet with you to discuss a recent matter and hear your perspective. Please do let me know if you have any difficulties attending. If you have any questions ahead of the meeting, please do not hesitate to get in touch. You are also welcome to bring a work colleague or trade union representative along with you if you would find that helpful. Kind regards, [Your name]
