If you are happy with progress

  • Tell them clearly and specifically what they are doing well. Give examples, not just "you're doing fine"
  • Acknowledge how they have settled in and the progress they have made
  • Build their confidence. They are still new and reassurance matters
  • Ask what support they still need going forward
  • Confirm the positive outcome in writing. Even a good review needs to be documented

If there are concerns

  • Name the concern clearly and specifically. Do not hint at it
  • Give the individual the opportunity to respond
  • Consider what support can be put in place. Training, mentoring, adjusted expectations
  • Set a clear improvement target with a timescale
  • Document everything in writing and give a copy to the individual
  • Before delivering an outcome: have concerns been raised early, has support been put in place and documented, and have those concerns continued despite that support?
